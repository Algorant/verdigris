# verdigris for hyprland

Compositor. Window borders and the share picker.

## install

Copy `verdigris.lua` into `~/.config/hypr/` and require it from your config.

## notes

Borders only; my window rules and bindings stay in my dotfiles.

No screenshot of its own: it shows only as borders around other windows, and the share picker exists only while a real screen share is being offered. The borders appear in the omarchy picture.
